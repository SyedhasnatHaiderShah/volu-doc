# 02 · Personas

**Status:** 🟢 Approved

Real personas, drawn from the UAE consumer and merchant landscape. Every feature should be validated against at least one of these. If a feature doesn't make one of these people's day better, we shouldn't build it.

---

## USER PERSONAS

### 👩‍💼 Leila — "The Dubai Professional"

- **Age:** 32
- **Role:** Senior marketing manager at a tech company in JLT
- **Income:** AED 28,000/month
- **Phone:** iPhone 15 Pro
- **Languages:** English (primary), Arabic (fluent)
- **Location:** Dubai Marina

**Her world:**
Leila lives a high-convenience life — Careem for rides, Talabat for food, Noon for everything else. She has Entertainer through her ENBD card but uses it maybe twice a month because the restaurants she actually wants aren't there. She went to Cobone once three years ago, got burned on a spa voucher the merchant refused, and never came back.

**What she wants from Volu:**
- Discover genuinely interesting new spots (spas, brunches, weekend activities)
- Save 30–50% on things she'd do anyway
- Zero friction: pay with Apple Pay, show QR, done
- Trust that when she arrives, the deal works

**What would lose her forever:**
- One rejected coupon at a merchant
- One hidden surcharge at redemption
- One support email that goes unanswered

**Her critical flows:**
- Browse "Near Me" on Thursday afternoon for weekend plans
- Check raffle entries once a week
- Gift a spa coupon to her mother on Mother's Day

---

### 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Faisal — "The Family Man"

- **Age:** 41
- **Role:** Finance director, mid-sized firm
- **Income:** AED 45,000/month
- **Phone:** Samsung Galaxy S24
- **Languages:** Arabic (primary), English (fluent)
- **Location:** Mirdif, Dubai

**His world:**
Three kids (ages 6, 9, 12), a wife who works part-time, and an endless parade of weekend activities, family meals, and school holidays to fill. Faisal lives on Google Maps and restaurant reviews. He's price-conscious but not cheap — he'd pay full price for quality, but deeply resents paying retail when a deal exists.

**What he wants from Volu:**
- Family-friendly filter that actually works (halal, kid-menu, parking)
- Multi-use coupons for things like kids' activity passes
- Arabic UI, WhatsApp notifications
- Bulk buy: 4 family tickets in one purchase

**What would lose him forever:**
- Deal that says "family-friendly" and isn't
- Needing to print anything
- English-only experience anywhere in the flow

**His critical flows:**
- Friday morning planning for the weekend's family outing
- Bulk buying entertainment vouchers before Eid
- Redeeming at a theme park with kids hanging off him

### 👩‍🎓 Rashmi — "The Value-Seeker Expat"

- **Age:** 26
- **Role:** Customer success associate, SaaS company
- **Income:** AED 9,500/month
- **Phone:** iPhone 13 (on instalments via Tabby)
- **Languages:** English, Hindi, basic Arabic
- **Location:** Al Barsha

**Her world:**
Rashmi is saving for her wedding next year, supporting family back home, and determined to enjoy Dubai without going broke. She knows every BNPL app, every promo code aggregator, every happy hour, every ladies' night. Cost matters more than convenience; she'll travel across the city for a 50% discount.

**What she wants from Volu:**
- Pay with Tabby / Tamara
- The biggest possible discount, transparently shown
- "Today only" urgency that makes her act
- Referral credit for signing up friends

**What would lose her forever:**
- Any deal where the all-in price after VAT surprises her
- BNPL not working at checkout
- Better discount on Cobone / Entertainer for the same thing

**Her critical flows:**
- Obsessive scrolling through Flash Deals at 9pm
- Referring 4 friends in one weekend for the credit
- Raffle: this is the hook she'll talk about

---

### 👨‍💼 Thomas — "The Tourist"

- **Age:** 38
- **Role:** Software architect visiting from Germany
- **Duration in UAE:** 7 days
- **Phone:** iPhone 14
- **Languages:** English, German
- **Location:** hotel in Downtown

**His world:**
On a week-long family holiday with his wife and two teenage kids. Hotel concierge gave them 20 brochures. He's researching experiences on TripAdvisor and GetYourGuide. He doesn't want membership, can't do annual subscriptions, and is wary of anything that looks scammy.

**What he wants from Volu:**
- No signup friction — download, buy, redeem
- Guest checkout option, or very fast sign-up with Apple ID
- Credit card accepted (non-local card)
- Experiences and activities, not spas
- Clear "tourist-friendly" tag / filter

**What would lose him forever:**
- Foreign card rejected at checkout (the Groupon UAE failure mode)
- Membership required to see the deal
- Unclear voucher terms

**His critical flows:**
- Thursday afternoon hotel-room purchase of Friday desert safari
- Scan-and-redeem at a dune camp with no WiFi

## MERCHANT PERSONAS

### 👩‍⚕️ Dr. Mona — "The Spa Owner"

- **Role:** Owner of a 3-branch wellness and medi-spa chain
- **Business size:** 35 staff, AED 450K monthly revenue
- **Tech comfort:** Medium — uses Excel, Instagram, a basic POS
- **Current deal platforms:** Cobone (regrets it), Instagram ads

**Her world:**
Dr. Mona runs three spas across Dubai. Her biggest pain is under-utilised midweek capacity (Tuesday/Wednesday slots are 40% full). She tried Cobone two years ago, was flooded with discount hunters who never returned, and was paid 60 days after redemption through opaque statements.

**What she wants from Volu:**
- Sell *specific* packages (midweek 1pm–5pm) to clear dead capacity
- See sold and redeemed in real time
- Cap daily redemptions so she's not overwhelmed
- Get paid within a week of each redemption
- Messages to past Volu customers to bring them back (with consent)

**What would lose her forever:**
- Opaque commission math
- A Volu customer posts a bad review when the cashier made a mistake
- Payout taking >14 days

**Her critical flows:**
- Creating a "Tuesday Reset" 90-min massage deal, capped at 30/day
- Checking the sales dashboard Tuesday morning
- Requesting a payout on the 15th of the month

---

### 👨‍🍳 Ahmed — "The Restaurant Operator"

- **Role:** GM of a casual-dining chain (4 branches)
- **Business size:** 80 staff across branches, AED 1.2M monthly revenue
- **Tech comfort:** High — fully digitized POS, inventory system, Talabat integration
- **Current deal platforms:** Talabat Dine-Out, Entertainer

**His world:**
Ahmed's margins are tight, kitchen throughput is maxed on weekends and empty on Monday–Wednesday lunch. Entertainer fills some seats but he's paying an annual listing fee regardless. Talabat Dine-Out works but they take a big cut.

**What he wants from Volu:**
- Multi-branch deal that lets customers choose their branch
- Set cover-limits per day per branch (he doesn't want 40 tables to show up at his 30-cover Jumeirah branch)
- Integrate with his POS so cashier doesn't need a second device
- Attract new customers, not cannibalise regulars

**What would lose him forever:**
- Confused customers wandering into the wrong branch
- Volu customer who takes a 50% coupon on top of a regular discount day
- POS staff juggling three apps

**His critical flows:**
- Creating a "Monday Lunch Mezze" deal valid at all 4 branches, capped at 15 covers per branch per day
- Validating a coupon in 3 seconds while a queue forms
- Pulling a Ramadan-month P&L from Volu's reports

---

### 👨‍💻 Karthik — "The Activity Operator"

- **Role:** Operations manager, desert safari + dhow cruise company
- **Business size:** 25 staff, high-ticket items (AED 250–900 per person)
- **Tech comfort:** Medium — website, basic booking system, WhatsApp for support
- **Current deal platforms:** Viator, GetYourGuide, Cobone

**His world:**
Karthik's business is 70% tourist, 30% resident. Capacity is fixed (a Land Cruiser seats 6, a dhow seats 60). Low-season months (July/August) are brutal. Cobone brings bookings but the support is non-existent and pickup locations often end up wrong in the voucher.

**What he wants from Volu:**
- Accurate pickup location data per deal (Volu validates this)
- Tourist-friendly filter so foreign buyers find him
- Bulk inventory management: "15 seats on Friday's 3pm cruise"
- Fast payout to cover fuel and driver costs

**What would lose him forever:**
- A buyer misses the dhow because the pickup location was wrong in Volu
- Overbooked trips because inventory sync is broken

**His critical flows:**
- Setting seat inventory by date for the next 30 days
- Cashier scans arriving guest's coupon at the pickup point
- Monthly reconciliation against his own booking system

## ADMIN PERSONAS

### 👨‍💼 Omar — "The Ops Admin"

- **Role:** Merchant success lead at Volu
- **Daily work:** KYC approvals, deal moderation, merchant support

**What he needs from the admin console:**
- KYC review with document preview and approve/reject/needs-info actions
- Deal approval queue with side-by-side preview as the user will see it
- Bulk operations for inviting / messaging merchants
- Audit trail of every action he took last week (for weekly ops review)

---

### 👩‍💻 Sarah — "The Finance Admin"

- **Role:** Finance associate at Volu
- **Daily work:** Payout approvals, VAT reporting, Stripe reconciliation

**What she needs:**
- Daily payout queue sorted by oldest pending
- Stripe vs internal ledger diff report with discrepancies flagged
- VAT monthly report in FTA-compatible format, one-click export
- Chargeback dispute evidence builder

---

### 👨‍🎨 Jana — "The Marketing Admin"

- **Role:** Growth marketer at Volu
- **Daily work:** Raffles, push campaigns, Meta/TikTok ads, influencer programs

**What she needs:**
- Raffle builder with rule testing (simulate entries before publishing)
- Push composer with audience segments, A/B testing, schedule
- "Promote this deal" one-click flow for Meta and TikTok
- ROAS dashboard tied to actual coupon sales
